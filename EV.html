EV Thoughts

Changes in thoughts before and after owning an electric vehicle

Background: We were exporing options for an additional family vehicle. We found a great deal for an EV priced the same as a comparable hybrid car. After hearing positive feedback from others who owned EVs, we decided to take the leap into the world of electric vehicles. We believed there was an opportunity to save money with less maintenance and fuel costs. So far, the adventure has been amazing!

Before vs After

Category Before Owning an EV After Owning an EV for 1.5 Years (26k miles)
Range and Charging
  • Limited range
  • Long time to charge
  • Limited locations to charge
  • Same beliefs as before but not as impactful as once believed
  • Able to support over 98% of driving, has become the primary car for driving
  • Charging costs half than equilvant gas cost. Convenient charging at home, but there is a learning curve on charging settings
  • Learning curve on factors affecting range like temperature and speed
Maintenance
  • No oil changes
  • Less parts to break
  • How often do brakes and tires need to be replaced? Article suggested tires might need to be replaced every 6k?!
  • No oil changes
  • Less parts to break
  • With regenerative braking assuming brakes will last longer
  • Still running on orginal tires, not sure when they will need to be replaced
  • Unknown costs if maintenance is required. Have only taken to dealership for free routine battery checks
Performance Can accelerate fast…
  • No lag in acceleration
  • Regenerative braking - effortless deceleration control
  • Instant climate control heat, no waiting for engine to warm up
Cold Weather Performance New articles in Jan 2024 of cold snap causing significant affect EVs not able to run or charge Cold weather cuts range significantly down range below 20 F degrees (note: my vehicle does not have heat pump but models with battery heating much less affected). Usually drive the gas car when close to 0 F degrees.
Environmental Impact
  • Environmental impact better than cars?
  • Do the batteries catch on fire?!
  • After searching, believe the environmental break even point is around 20k miles
  • Manufacturer suggested battery was designed to last 300k miles
  • Bidirectional charging capabilities: Considering as possible alternative to emergency gas generator
  • No engine to turn off (getting used to this makes me at risk of forgetting to turn off my gas car…)
  • EV stays outside garage in unlikely event of a fire but less concerned on the EV catching fire. Ironically have increased sensitivity on the flammability of gas

Savings at 26,000 Miles

Assumptions / Notes:

  • Gas vehicle: 26 mpg, Oil Change every 4,000 miles
  • Electric vehicle: 3.5 mpkw, No maintenance required, Additional registration fee of $100
  • Oil change cost: $50 per service (assuming 6 services)
  • Fuel costs: $3.00 per gallon (fluctuated between $3.50 to $2.50), $0.23 per kWh (home charging only)

Cost Summary:

  • Fuel Savings: $3,000 - $1,709 = $1,291
  • Oil Change Savings: $300 - $0 = $300
  • Additional EV Registration Fee = - ($100)
Total Savings: $1,491



Overall: I wouldn't rely solely on an EV without a gas vehicle as backup, but really enjoy the convenience of charging at home, cost savings, and speed control.
